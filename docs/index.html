Tennis match videos, scored and cut

GameSetAI is a local app for tennis clubs and players. You upload a match video, mark or auto-detect points, keep score, and export a highlight reel with an optional scoreboard.

What it does

  • Upload from this computer or Google Drive
  • Score points on a timeline (manual or auto-detect rallies)
  • Share a link so someone can fix the score without an account
  • Export clips with a scoreboard overlay

Video files stay on the computer running GameSetAI. Accounts use email sign-in. Google Drive access is only used when you choose to import a video from Drive.
