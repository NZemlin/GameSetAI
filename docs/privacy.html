Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

GameSetAI is a local tennis video scoring app operated by Nicholas Zemlin (nicholaszemlin@gmail.com). This policy describes how the app handles information.

Account

If you create an account, we store your email address and profile (display name and player/club role) using Supabase Auth. This is used to sign you in and to keep your matches attached to your account.

Match videos and scores

Videos you upload are stored on the computer running GameSetAI so they can be scored and exported. Match setup, point times, and share links are stored with your account. We do not sell this data.

Google Drive

If you choose Upload from Google Drive, GameSetAI asks Google for permission to see and download your Google Drive files (the drive.readonly scope). We use that access only to download the video you pick or whose Drive link you paste. We do not use Drive files to train AI models, show ads, or share them with other apps. The downloaded video is saved on your computer. You can revoke access at Google Account → Third-party access.

Share links

If you create a share link, anyone with that link can open the match, adjust scoring, and export. Do not send a share link to people who should not see the video.

Changes

If how we use Google user data changes, we will update this page. Questions: nicholaszemlin@gmail.com.
